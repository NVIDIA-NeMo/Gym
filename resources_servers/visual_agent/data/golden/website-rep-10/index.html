05 · GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered

Can I leave and come back in?

Yes. Wristbands allow re-entry until 22:00 each day.

Is the festival cashless?

All bars and food stalls take cards and phones. Top-up points are at both gates.

What can I bring in?

Sealed soft drinks up to 1 litre, empty reusable bottles and small bags. No glass.

Are children welcome?

Under-12s go free with a paying adult. Ear defenders are free at First aid.
