Lemon Ricotta Pancakes with Blueberry Compote
Tall, tender pancakes lifted with whipped ricotta and bright lemon, finished with a quick maple blueberry compote. Weekend-worthy, weekday-easy.

Method

Step 1
Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
✓
Step 2
In a second bowl, whisk the ricotta, milk, eggs, lemon zest and lemon juice until smooth.
3
Step 3
Fold the wet ingredients into the dry until just combined, then rest the batter.

4
Step 4
