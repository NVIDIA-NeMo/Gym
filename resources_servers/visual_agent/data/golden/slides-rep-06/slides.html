BALANCE OF RISKS TO THE CPI PROJECTION
POLICY DECISION

The Committee voted by a majority of 6–3 to maintain Bank Rate at 3.75%. Three members preferred a 25 bp cut, citing the rise in unemployment to 4.8% and weaker pay growth.

Policy will need to remain restrictive for longer if services inflation, now 4.6%, fails to ease as projected.
