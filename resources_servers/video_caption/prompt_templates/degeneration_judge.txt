Determine whether the input text is a well-formed, non-degenerated video caption.

# Caption Quality Evaluation
1. **No degeneration or excessive repetition.** The caption remains meaningful throughout, without repetitive loops, excessive restatement of the same information, or breakdown into garbled or disconnected text.
2. **Coherent and relevant video description.** The caption is understandable, logically organized, internally consistent, and focused on describing the video. It does not drift into unrelated commentary or filler.
3. **No exposed reasoning or drafting.** The caption presents a finished description directly. It does not include the writer's thinking process, deliberation about what to say, or commentary about composing or revising the answer.

Evaluate writing quality, not factual accuracy or coverage of video events. Length alone is not a failure, and coherent descriptions of repeated actions are acceptable.
Return pass if the caption meets these criteria; return fail if it clearly violates any of them.

# Output Format
Return only a JSON object, either {"verdict": "pass"} or {"verdict": "fail"}. Do not include explanations, additional fields, or Markdown fences.
