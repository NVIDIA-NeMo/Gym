## Investir Project Introduction and Goals

**Investir** is a command-line utility, written in Python and built on Typer/Click, to analyse account activity exported as CSV files from share investing platforms (Freetrade and Trading 212). Its primary purpose is to produce the figures needed to fill out the UK Capital Gains SA108 form for a Self Assessment tax return, computing gain or loss per disposal following the HMRC share identification rules (same-day matching, bed & breakfasting within 30 days, and the Section 104 pool). It can also display share buy/sell orders, dividends paid out, interest earned on cash, cash deposits/withdrawals, and open holdings with cost, allocation weight and unrealised gain/loss.

Additional goals:

- Take into account share sub-division and share consolidation (split) events, fetched from Yahoo Finance, when normalising share quantities.
- Support orders not realised in pound sterling by converting monetary values using historical exchange rates sourced from Yahoo Finance, HMRC's monthly published rates, or a local CSV file specified by the user.
- Provide text, CSV, JSON and HTML output formats, and filters by tax year and ticker.
- Cache downloaded securities information and exchange rates on local disk so that reports can be generated offline.
