# VoxLingua107

Spoken language identification on the VoxLingua107 development set: the model hears a clip and answers with one
language code (top-1 accuracy).

- Source: [VoxLingua107](https://cs.taltech.ee/staff/tanel.alumae/data/voxlingua107/) development set, `dev.zip`
  (444 MB), SHA-256 `54c352d2ecbcbc03a4bb24151750a287573a5c2738636387a2240700bb89d2d2`. Paper: Valk and Alumäe, SLT
  2021. License: CC-BY-4.0 (dataset page); the copyright of the clips remains with the original video owners.
- The archive holds 1,609 clips in 33 languages, validated by at least two volunteers; preparation checks its hash and
  per-language census and writes about 0.9 GB of audio besides the archive.
- Default: **all 33 languages (1,609 clips)**; the prompt lists the 33 codes (generated by `prepare()` into each row's
  source prompt) and the answer must match the reference exactly. A 24-language subset
  (`LANGUAGES_24`, 1,259 clips) is also available: `ar da de el en es et fi fr hr hu it ja lt lv nl pl pt ru sl sv tr uk zh`; the nine
  others (`az fa hy is mk nn no sr ur`, 350 clips) are left out and the prompt lists the 24 codes. Accuracy over 33
  languages is not comparable with the 24-language numbers:

  ```bash
  gym eval prepare --benchmark voxlingua107 \
      '+prepare_script_args.languages=[ar,da,de,el,en,es,et,fi,fr,hr,hu,it,ja,lt,lv,nl,pl,pt,ru,sl,sv,tr,uk,zh]'
  ```

- Metrics (`audio/voxlingua107/pass@1/*`): `accuracy_micro` over all clips, `accuracy_macro` over the languages
  present, and `accuracy::<language>`.
- Run: see the [audio_eval README](../../resources_servers/audio_eval/README.md#preparing-and-running-an-audio-benchmark);
  a smoke run still downloads and verifies the archive in full (the first clips in language order belong to one
  language).
